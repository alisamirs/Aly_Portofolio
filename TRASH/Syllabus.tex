\subsection{Detailed study guide}\label{detailed-study-guide}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  The UK Tax system and its administration

  \begin{enumerate}
  \def\labelenumii{\arabic{enumii}.}
  \tightlist
  \item
    The overall function and purpose of taxation in a modern economy

    \begin{itemize}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Describe the purpose (economic, social etc) of taxation in a
      modern economy
    \item
      Explain the difference between direct and indirect taxation
    \item
      Identify the different types of capital and revenue tax.
    \end{itemize}
  \item
    Principal sources of revenue law and practice

    \begin{itemize}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Describe the overall structure of the UK tax system
    \item
      State the different sources of revenue law
    \item
      Describe the organisation HM Revenue \& Customs (HMRC) and its
      terms of reference
    \item
      Appreciate the interaction of the UK tax system with that of other
      tax jurisdictions
    \item
      Appreciate the need for double taxation agreements
    \item
      Explain the need for an ethical and professional approach
    \end{itemize}
  \item
    The systems for self-assessment and the making of returns

    \begin{itemize}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Explain and apply the features of the self-assessment system as it
      applies to individuals
    \item
      Explain and apply the features of the self-assessment system as it
      applies to companies, including the use of iXBRL
    \end{itemize}
  \item
    The time limits for the submission of information, claims and
    payment of tax, including payments on account

    \begin{itemize}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Recognise the time limits that apply to the filing of returns and
      the making of claims
    \item
      Recognise the due dates for the payment of tax under the
      self-assessment system, and compute payments on account and
      balancing payments/repayments for individuals
    \item
      Explain how large companies are required to account for
      corporation tax on a quarterly basis and compute the quarterly
      instalment payments
    \item
      List the information and records that taxpayers need to retain for
      tax purposes
    \end{itemize}
  \item
    The procedures relating to compliance checks, appeals and disputes

    \begin{itemize}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Explain the circumstances in which HM Revenue \& Customs can make
      a compliance check into a self-assessment tax return
    \item
      Explain the procedures for dealing with appeals and First and
      Upper Tier Tribunals
    \end{itemize}
  \item
    Penalties for non-compliance

    \begin{itemize}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Calculate late payment interest and state the penalties that can
      be charged
    \end{itemize}
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
