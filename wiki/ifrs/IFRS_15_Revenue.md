# IFRS 15 â€” Revenue from Contracts with Customers

IFRS 15 provides a principles-based model for recognising revenue from contracts with customers.

## Five-step model

The study sequence is to identify the contract, identify performance obligations, determine the transaction price, allocate that price, and recognise revenue when or as performance obligations are satisfied.
