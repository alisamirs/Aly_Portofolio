IFRS 10 uses control as the basis for determining when an investor consolidates an investee.

## Key ideas

The control assessment considers power over the investee, exposure or rights to variable returns, and the ability to use power to affect those returns. Consolidation then requires combining the relevant financial information and eliminating intragroup effects.
