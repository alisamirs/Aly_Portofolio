IAS 37 distinguishes provisions from contingent liabilities and contingent assets and sets recognition and measurement principles.

## Key ideas

A provision requires a present obligation from a past event, a probable outflow, and a reliable estimate. The standard also guides disclosure where recognition criteria are not met.
