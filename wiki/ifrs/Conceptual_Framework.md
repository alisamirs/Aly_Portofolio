# Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting

The Conceptual Framework describes the objective of general purpose financial reporting and the qualitative characteristics of useful financial information. It supports consistent standard-setting and helps preparers develop accounting policies when no specific Standard applies.

## Core themes

The framework connects reporting objectives, the reporting entity, elements of financial statements, recognition and derecognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure.
