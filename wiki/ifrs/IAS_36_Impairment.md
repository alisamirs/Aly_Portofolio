IAS 36 sets out when assets and cash-generating units are tested for impairment and how recoverable amount is determined.

## Key ideas

The main concepts are impairment indicators, value in use, fair value less costs of disposal, cash-generating units, allocation of impairment losses, and reversal restrictions.
