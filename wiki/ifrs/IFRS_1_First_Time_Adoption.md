IFRS 1 establishes the framework for an entity preparing its first financial statements that explicitly and unreservedly comply with IFRS Accounting Standards.

## Key ideas

The adoption process centres on a date of transition, an opening IFRS statement of financial position, consistent accounting policies, and transparent reconciliations from previous GAAP. IFRS 1 combines a general retrospective principle with specific optional exemptions and mandatory exceptions.
